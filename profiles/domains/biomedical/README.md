# Biomedical and clinical domain profile

This profile captures figure conventions for clinical, diagnostic, and biomarker research.

## Key principles

- Report observation units and replicate structure.
- Separate discrimination, calibration, and threshold utility.
- Show censoring and number at risk in survival curves.
- Preserve pair identity in before/after or matched designs.

## Preferred families

- `comparison_effect` for group comparisons.
- `distribution_uncertainty` for biomarker distributions.
- `paired_operating_point` for matched clinical measurements.
- `classification_diagnostics` for diagnostic model evaluation.
- `statistical_discovery` for biomarker screening.

## Reporting standards

- TRIPOD+AI for prediction models.
- CONSORT for trials.
- REMARK for biomarker studies.
