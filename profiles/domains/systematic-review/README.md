# Systematic review and meta-analysis domain profile

This profile captures figure conventions for systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

## Key principles

- Follow PRISMA study flow and report reasons for exclusion.
- Forest plots must show effect sizes, confidence intervals, and study weights.
- Summarize risk of bias across included studies.

## Preferred families

- `network_flow_set` for PRISMA flow diagrams.
- `comparison_effect` for summary effects.
- `statistical_discovery` for sensitivity and subgroup analyses.
- `mechanism_architecture` for conceptual summaries.

## Reporting standards

- PRISMA 2020 for systematic reviews.
- MOOSE for meta-analyses of observational studies.
