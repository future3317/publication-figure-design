# Figure Legend Contract

Build the legend from verified figure facts; do not invent statistics.

Use this order unless the target journal specifies otherwise:

1. `Fig. N |` followed by a short declarative title.
2. One sentence per panel in label order, stating what is shown and defining symbols or encodings that are not obvious.
3. For each quantitative claim: `n` and replicate definition, center, spread/interval, statistical test, multiple-comparison correction, and exact comparison.
4. Source-data statement when applicable.

Preserve canonical capitalization such as gene, protein, model, and method names. Display labels may start with an initial capital; do not blindly title-case canonical identifiers.

Define error bars or bands explicitly (`SD`, `SEM`, `95% CI`, seed SD, fold interval, and so on). Do not describe uncertainty that the panel does not show. Apply a journal-specific word or character limit only after verifying the current author guidance.
