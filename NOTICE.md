## Composition and upstream attribution

Publication Figure Design is an integration of three upstream skill/code lineages plus
new work in this repository:

1. **academic-figure-skill** ([TingxiYu/academic-figure-skill](https://github.com/TingxiYu/academic-figure-skill)) — the original academic-figure skill layout, cross-tool installation adapters, and baseline figure workflow. The upstream repository's terms remain relevant to material derived from it.
2. **figures4papers** ([ChenLiu-1996/figures4papers](https://github.com/ChenLiu-1996/figures4papers)) — publication-style and semantic-encoding ideas used as documented design inspiration. Its source images and plotting checkout are not bundled in this public repository.
3. **nature-skills / nature-figure** ([Yuan1z0825/nature-skills](https://github.com/Yuan1z0825/nature-skills), Apache-2.0) — adapted helper patterns and the Apache-2.0 visual-reference lineage used by the source-reconstruction catalog.

The **future3317/publication-figure-design** contribution is the integration and
optimization layer: concrete-reference reconstruction contracts, palette and contrast
gates, structural-optimization checks, source-reference catalogs, independently rendered
synthetic redraws, journal delivery contracts, and the unified QA workflow.

## Bundled-material boundary

- Figure-generation code and synthetic redraws are original project materials or are
  explicitly marked in their metadata.
- The public visual catalog contains only the reviewed Apache-2.0 `nature-figure`
  sources and independent reconstructions listed by the machine-readable manifests.
- Unknown-license source images, private screenshots, and development-only reference
  material are intentionally excluded from this distribution.
- A visual reference is an inspiration and QA asset, not a license to reuse source-paper
  data, labels, or artwork in a manuscript.
